Your data never leaves this page.

The CSVs are read and the .xlsx report is built entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or sent to any server — a Content-Security-Policy on this page blocks all outbound connections. Your edits are saved only in this browser (see 🍪). You can even save this page and run it offline.

Storage & cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies and sends nothing to any server. To remember your work, it uses your browser's own local storage (which — unlike cookies — is never transmitted anywhere).

When you edit a report, your actions, primary choices, removed records and column setup are saved locally, keyed to the uploaded file. Re-upload the same file after a reload and your changes come back. It stays on this device until you clear it.

How to use this site
  1. Upload the export CSV (left). First row = headings, records below. The report builds instantly, in your browser.
  2. Read the dashboard — group counts and records read, plus any data warnings.
  3. Browse the three tabs — Likely duplicates, Problematic duplicates, Unclassified. Records are grouped by domain; the primary is the bold top row. Account ID links to Salesforce.
  4. Add an Opportunities CSV (right, optional) to get an Opportunities column — a bubble with the count and how long ago the newest one changed; click it for the full list.
  5. 👁 eye button on any row opens that group in Edit actions.
  6. ⚙ Customize data — drag fields between Displayed and Hidden, reorder them, rename with the pencil (✎), or revert names. This drives the view and the download.
  7. ✎ Edit actions — step through groups: pick the primary (radio), choose Merge / Ignore / Evaluate / None, add remarks, and (for groups with 3+ records) remove records that don't belong. Unclassified needs both an action and a primary — except Ignore, which needs no primary. Move with ← / →, finish with Esc.
  8. ⬇ Download (.xlsx) — a formatted workbook with all your choices baked in.

Your edits are remembered on this device (see 🍪) and come back when you re-upload the same file.
